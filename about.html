About

A practical gear site with a little dungeon-light personality.

Gadget Goblin exists to help readers find useful tech without pretending every shiny thing is a must-buy.

What the site covers

Useful gadgets, budget gaming gear, PC upgrades, desk setup upgrades, power gear, weird but practical tech, hot product roundups, and research-based product recommendations.

How picks are handled

Reviews are labeled by type. Research-based reviews use public specs, official documentation, reputable coverage, owner-pattern research, and clear tradeoff analysis. Hands-on claims should only be added after real testing.

How the site earns

Gadget Goblin may earn commissions from qualifying purchases when readers buy through affiliate links. Editorial notes should stay useful first and monetized second.
