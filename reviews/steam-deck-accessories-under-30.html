Buying criteria

  • For charging gear, verify USB-C wattage and safety reputation.
  • For cases and grips, check vent clearance and trigger protection.
  • For stands, look for stability and a viewing angle that works with a controller.
  • Avoid accessories that make the Deck harder to cool, charge, or pack.

Common mistakes

  • Buying the cheapest charger without checking output.
  • Adding bulky grips before deciding whether portability matters.
  • Using an adapter that stresses the USB-C port.
  • Forgetting that many accessories also need to fit OLED or LCD model differences.

FAQ

Should I buy the cheapest USB-C charger?

No. Check output wattage and safety reputation before using a charger with an expensive handheld.

Do I need a dock first?

Only if you plan to use the Steam Deck like a desktop or TV console. Otherwise, cable, case, and stand upgrades are usually more useful under $30.
