Buying criteria

  • Measure desk thickness before buying clamp-on accessories.
  • Prioritize cable routing, monitor height, and power access.
  • Avoid accessories that reduce mouse space.
  • Use lighting to reduce eye strain, not just to glow.

Common mistakes

  • Buying decorative gear before solving cables.
  • Adding a huge mousepad that covers cable exits.
  • Forgetting clearance under the desk.
  • Using weak adhesive clips on dusty surfaces.

Final take

For a small setup, buy upgrades that reclaim space or remove friction. Cable routing, monitor height, and power access beat decorative clutter.

FAQ

What should I upgrade first on a small desk?

Start with cable management and monitor height. Those two changes usually make the setup easier to use every day.
